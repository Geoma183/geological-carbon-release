# Accounting boundaries and terminology

This document defines release-time accounting governance; it does not claim that every described component has already been independently calculated or validated.

## Core accounting boundary

A harmonized long-term account for direct geological-carbon CO₂ emissions from specified sources. Document the resource scope, gases, territorial allocation, versions, activity data, conversion factors, exclusions, and any reconciliations before publication.

## Extended-boundary corrections

Investigate extraction, fugitive methane and other gases, processing, storage, transport, non-energy uses, product end of life, and carbonation-related uptake only where there is an identifiable physical process, compatible data, and a traceable calculation.

Distinguish all of the following:

- **Fossil-fuel extended lifecycle emissions** versus **carbonate-mineral extended lifecycle net emissions**.
- **Gross emissions** versus **net emissions**.
- **Direct emissions** versus **supply-chain emissions**.
- **Production responsibility** versus **consumption responsibility**.
- **CO₂** versus **CO₂e**.

Do not add accounting boundaries or trade-related responsibility accounts mechanically. Document overlapping processes and any exclusion/assignment rule.

## Carbonation uptake

If included, document the production cohort, material type, use phase, demolition phase, time profile, assumed uptake factors, and any mismatch between place of production and place of carbonation. Distinguish a modeled uptake from a directly measured atmospheric sink.

## Evidence status

Records must distinguish observed values, official statistics, historical estimates, reconstructed/model-based estimates, interpolated/extrapolated estimates, missing values, and not-applicable values.

**Missing is not zero.** Report imputation, temporal bridging, cross-country mapping, and calibration decisions.

## Reconciliation requirements

For each released variable or series, declare its equation, gas and unit, geography, temporal coverage, resource/process inclusion, uncertainty/limitations, production/consumption basis, gross/net status, source and version, and permitted redistribution status.
