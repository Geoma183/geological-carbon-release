## Proposed description (for use only after dataset scope is validated)

This dataset accompanies research on anthropogenic release from geological carbon reservoirs since the Industrial Revolution. Its planned scope comprises annual global and selected-country accounts for coal, oil, natural gas and carbonate-mineral sources; direct geological-carbon CO2 and explicitly separated lifecycle-boundary emissions; documented historical reconstructions; modern direct-CO2 Kaya–LMDI accounting decompositions; and selected uncertainty and validation outputs.

The historical series will distinguish original observations, official statistics, historical estimates, model reconstructions, interpolation/extrapolation, missing values and non-applicable values. Gross and net fluxes, direct and supply-chain emissions, production- and consumption-based responsibility, and CO2 and CO2-equivalent are documented separately. The submitted manuscript and finalized supporting information will determine which exact files and dates form the archived version. Some underlying third-party inputs may require retrieval from their original providers.
